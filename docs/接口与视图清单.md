# 接口与视图清单（学生 1-5）

## 1. 认证（学生 1）

| URL | 名称 | 方法 | 视图 | 说明 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `/accounts/login/` | `login` | GET/POST | `LoginView` | 登录页，支持演示账号一键填入 |
| `/accounts/signup/` | `signup` | GET/POST | `system_views.signup` | 用户注册，默认只读访客 |
| `/accounts/logout/` | `logout` | POST | `LogoutView` | 退出登录，CSRF 保护 |

## 2. 工作台（全模块）

| URL | 名称 | 方法 | 视图 | 功能 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `/` | `dashboard` | GET | `views.dashboard` | 待处理/待执行/今日完成/库存批次；模块入口；最近入库/移库/出库；低库存；操作动态 |

## 3. 用户、仓库、供应商、权限（学生 1）

| URL | 名称 | 方法 | 视图 | 权限 | 功能 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `/users/` | `user_list` | GET | `system_views.user_list` | `user.manage` | 用户筛选、分页、岗位统计 |
| `/users/new/` | `user_create` | GET/POST | `system_views.user_create` | `user.manage` | 创建用户、分配岗位 |
| `/users/<pk>/edit/` | `user_edit` | GET/POST | `system_views.user_edit` | `user.manage` | 编辑岗位、启停、重置密码 |
| `/warehouses/` | `warehouse_list` | GET | `system_views.warehouse_list` | `warehouse.manage` | 仓库卡片 + 库位明细 |
| `/warehouses/new/` | `warehouse_create` | GET/POST | `system_views.warehouse_create` | `warehouse.manage` | 新建仓库 |
| `/warehouses/<pk>/edit/` | `warehouse_edit` | GET/POST | `system_views.warehouse_edit` | `warehouse.manage` | 编辑仓库 |
| `/warehouses/<pk>/delete/` | `warehouse_delete` | POST | `system_views.warehouse_delete` | `warehouse.manage` | 删除空仓库（有关联则拒绝） |
| `/locations/new/` | `location_create` | GET/POST | `system_views.location_create` | `warehouse.manage` | 新建库位，可带 `?warehouse=` |
| `/locations/<pk>/edit/` | `location_edit` | GET/POST | `system_views.location_edit` | `warehouse.manage` | 编辑库位 |
| `/locations/<pk>/delete/` | `location_delete` | POST | `system_views.location_delete` | `warehouse.manage` | 删除空库位 |
| `/suppliers/` | `supplier_list` | GET | `system_views.supplier_list` | `supplier.manage` | 供应商列表与筛选 |
| `/suppliers/new/` | `supplier_create` | GET/POST | `system_views.supplier_create` | `supplier.manage` | 新建供应商 |
| `/suppliers/<pk>/edit/` | `supplier_edit` | GET/POST | `system_views.supplier_edit` | `supplier.manage` | 编辑供应商 |
| `/suppliers/<pk>/delete/` | `supplier_delete` | POST | `system_views.supplier_delete` | `supplier.manage` | 删除无入库记录的供应商 |
| `/settings/roles/` | `role_permissions` | GET/POST | `system_views.role_permissions` | `role.manage` | 配置菜单与接口权限，`?role=` 切换角色 |

## 4. 药材与库存（学生 2）

| URL | 名称 | 方法 | 视图 | 权限 | 功能 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `/herbs/` | `herb_list` | GET | `catalog_views.herb_list` | `herb.view` | 关键字/类别/状态筛选、分页、库存汇总 |
| `/herbs/new/` | `herb_create` | GET/POST | `catalog_views.herb_create` | `herb.manage` | 新增药材 |
| `/herbs/<pk>/` | `herb_detail` | GET | `catalog_views.herb_detail` | `herb.view` | 药材信息、批次、流水 |
| `/herbs/<pk>/edit/` | `herb_edit` | GET/POST | `catalog_views.herb_edit` | `herb.manage` | 编辑药材 |
| `/herbs/<pk>/delete/` | `herb_delete` | POST | `catalog_views.herb_delete` | `herb.manage` | 删除无业务引用的药材 |
| `/stock/` | `stock_list` | GET | `catalog_views.stock_list` | `stock.view` | 批次筛选、低库存、分页 |
| `/stock/new/` | `stock_create` | GET/POST | `catalog_views.stock_create` | `stock.manage` | 新建批次 + 期初流水 |
| `/stock/<pk>/` | `stock_detail` | GET | `catalog_views.stock_detail` | `stock.view` | 批次库存、流水 |
| `/stock/<pk>/edit/` | `stock_edit` | GET/POST | `catalog_views.stock_edit` | `stock.manage` | 维护日期/数量，数量变化写 ADJUST |
| `/stock/<pk>/delete/` | `stock_delete` | POST | `catalog_views.stock_delete` | `stock.manage` | 删除零库存、无流水空批次 |

## 5. 入库管理（学生 3）

| URL | 名称 | 方法 | 视图 | 权限 | 功能 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `/inbound/` | `inbound_list` | GET | `inbound_views.inbound_list` | `inbound.view` | 状态、供应商、日期、关键字筛选与分页 |
| `/inbound/new/` | `inbound_create` | GET/POST | `inbound_views.inbound_create` | `inbound.create` | 表头 + formset 多明细，保存/提交 |
| `/inbound/<pk>/` | `inbound_detail` | GET | `inbound_views.inbound_detail` | `inbound.view` | 明细、合计、状态、轨迹、验收/入库操作 |
| `/inbound/<pk>/edit/` | `inbound_edit` | GET/POST | `inbound_views.inbound_edit` | `inbound.create` | 编辑草稿/驳回单明细 |
| `/inbound/<pk>/submit/` | `inbound_submit` | POST | `inbound_views.inbound_submit` | `inbound.create` | 草稿/驳回 → 待验收 |
| `/inbound/<pk>/approve/` | `inbound_approve` | POST | `inbound_views.inbound_approve` | `inbound.audit` | 验收通过 |
| `/inbound/<pk>/reject/` | `inbound_reject` | POST | `inbound_views.inbound_reject` | `inbound.audit` | 驳回，原因必填 |
| `/inbound/<pk>/complete/` | `inbound_complete` | POST | `inbound_views.inbound_complete` | `inbound.execute` | 逐条增库存 + INBOUND 流水 |
| `/inbound/<pk>/cancel/` | `inbound_cancel` | POST | `inbound_views.inbound_cancel` | 申请人/主管 | 取消未完成单 |

## 6. 移库 / 出库（学生 4）

| `/transfers/<pk>/submit/` | `transfer_submit` | POST | `transfer.create` | 提交审核 |
| `/transfers/<pk>/approve/` | `transfer_approve` | POST | `transfer.audit` | 审核通过 |
| `/transfers/<pk>/reject/` | `transfer_reject` | POST | `transfer.audit` | 驳回 |
| `/transfers/<pk>/complete/` | `transfer_complete` | POST | `transfer.execute` | 执行移库 |
| `/transfers/<pk>/cancel/` | `transfer_cancel` | POST | 申请人/主管 | 取消 |
